iBOK Individual 

iBOK, the advanced online banking service from the Bank of Khartoum, is designed to meet the specific needs of our clients. IBOK offers a comprehensive range of digital banking tools that simplify financial management and enhance efficiency. It integrates seamlessly with your current systems and features an intuitive interface that requires minimal to no training. Our customer support team is always available to assist with any questions or technical issues you may have. The Bank of Khartoum's personal online banking service provides a secure way to manage your accounts online. You can check balances, make transactions, and stay connected to your account anytime, anywhere—whether at home or in the office—24/7.  

Features

  • Account Balance Inquiry 
  • Download/View the Account statement
  • Fund Transfer (Own, Other BOK accounts)
  • Manage Beneficiary
  • Update Profile 
Benefits

With IBOK, you can benefit from the following features:

  • 24/7 Access.
  • Secure Transactions.
  • Real-Time Monitoring.
  • Automated Payments.  
How to Apply

If you are already registered for Bankak Mobile Banking, you can start using iBOK Individual right away.  

  • Go to the iBOK platform and enter your CIF number and password.
  • For security, a One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered mobile on login and once you initiate the transactions.
  • Enter the OTP to log in and approve your transactions.

This two-step process ensures your account and transactions are protected.
